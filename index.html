About This Calculator

The Problem

Medical emergencies create sudden financial stress. Traditional EMI systems use fixed monthly payments without considering the patient's recovery time or reduced income after surgery. This increases financial pressure during a physically and emotionally difficult period.

Our Solution

The Pay-As-You-Recover system provides a flexible repayment plan that:

  • Adjusts EMI based on your income capacity (capped at 35%)
  • Provides grace period equal to your recovery time
  • Assesses financial risk to help you plan better
  • Reduces repayment stress during recovery

How It Works

1. Grace Period: No payments during recovery. Grace period = Recovery weeks ÷ 4 (converted to months)

2. Affordable EMI: Maximum EMI is capped at 35% of your monthly income based on standard affordability guidelines

3. Risk Assessment: Evaluated based on surgery cost vs income, recovery duration, and savings buffer
